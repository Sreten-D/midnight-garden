**Hello, I'm Sreten, and welcome to my garden.**

I'm from Serbia, and I'm curious about almost everything. My interests are wide and a little untidy: money and freedom, machines that might think, the smallest distance in the universe, and a long list of things I still want to learn or try. When something catches my attention, I start asking questions, and I rarely stop at the first answer.

Here is my favorite daydream. If I were completely free of the need to work for a salary, my days would fill up quickly. I would learn about the stars, watch birds and wildlife, get to know the plants I walk past, study art, and write a book instead of a blog. I would live in a beach house on an island, or a cabin on a mountain near a lake, perhaps with a small farm full of animals. That is my idea of a perfect life, and it is only the short list. The real one is much longer.

Until then, this site is where my questions end up. It is a digital garden, not a magazine. Some pieces are polished, some are still growing, and some will change as I learn more. I research in my free time, I write from my own views and beliefs, I cite my sources, and I say plainly when I am guessing.

I write to express myself: to take what is inside me and shape it into good prose. Writing is how I find out what I actually think.

If something here makes you think, doubt or disagree, I would like to hear it. A good debate is the best compliment a text can get.

Sreten
