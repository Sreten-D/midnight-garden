**Hello, I'm Sreten, and welcome to my garden.**

I'm from Serbia, and I'm curious about almost everything. My interests are wide and a little untidy, but they keep returning to the same few places. I look up at the stars and wonder where it all began, why there is something rather than nothing, and what might wait beyond the edge of everything we can know. I look around at the living world of plants, animals and wild places. I look inward, at how the mind works, why we believe what we believe, and what philosophy has made of it all.

I think about how machines have changed our lives, and how one day they might even think for themselves. Although I often long for older, simpler times, I know that technological progress cannot be stopped: it is in our nature to ask, to create and, in the end, to adapt. Other things fascinate me as well, among them the origin and uses of money, though only for the time it buys me to chase everything else. When something catches my attention, I start asking questions, and I rarely stop at the first answer.

My daydream is a life in which the hours I spend working for a salary no longer decide how my days are spent. They would fill up quickly. I would learn about the stars, watch birds and wildlife, and get to know the plants I walk past. I would study art, learn to play an instrument, read philosophy and psychology, and write a book, among many other things. I would live in a beach house on an island, or in a cabin on a mountain beside a lake, perhaps with a small farm full of animals. That is my idea of a perfect life, and it is only the short list. The real one is much longer.

Until then, this site is where my questions end up. It is a digital garden, not a magazine. Some pieces are polished, some are still growing, and some will change as I learn more. I research in my free time, write from my own views and beliefs, cite my sources, and say plainly when I am guessing.

I write to express myself: to take what is inside me and shape it into good prose with a purpose. Writing is how I find out what I actually think.

If something here makes you think, doubt or disagree, I would like to hear it. A good debate is the best compliment a text can get.

Sreten
