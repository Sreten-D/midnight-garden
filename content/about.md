**Hello, I'm Sreten, and welcome to my garden.**

I'm from Serbia, and I'm curious about almost everything. My interests are wide and a little untidy, but they keep returning to the same few places. I look up at the stars and wonder about the smallest distance the universe allows. I look around at the living world of plants, birds and wild places. I look inward, at how the mind works, why we believe what we believe, and what philosophy has made of it all. Machines that might one day think belong somewhere in that picture, and so does money, though last, and only as a means. For me it is a vehicle, never the destination: a way to buy the freedom and time for everything else. When something catches my attention, I start asking questions, and I rarely stop at the first answer.

Here is my favorite daydream. If I were completely free of the need to work for a salary, my days would fill up quickly. I would learn about the stars, watch birds and wildlife, and get to know the plants I walk past. I would study art, read philosophy and psychology without hurrying, and write a book instead of a blog. I would live in a beach house on an island, or in a cabin on a mountain beside a lake, perhaps with a small farm full of animals. That is my idea of a perfect life, and it is only the short list. The real one is much longer.

Until then, this site is where my questions end up. It is a digital garden, not a magazine. Some pieces are polished, some are still growing, and some will change as I learn more. I research in my free time, write from my own views and beliefs, cite my sources, and say plainly when I am guessing.

I write to express myself: to take what is inside me and shape it into good prose. Writing is how I find out what I actually think.

If something here makes you think, doubt or disagree, I would like to hear it. A good debate is the best compliment a text can get.

Sreten
